Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

1. Overview

CodeHobo QBO Connectors ("the Application") is a private, internal-use tool operated by CodeHobo. It is not a public-facing product and is not available for use by third parties. This Privacy Policy describes how the Application handles data accessed through the QuickBooks Online (QBO) API.

2. Data Accessed

The Application accesses the following categories of data from QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API:

  • Accounting records (invoices, journal entries, payments, accounts)
  • Customer and vendor records associated with business accounts
  • Financial reports and transaction history

This data is accessed solely to support internal accounting and financial reporting operations.

3. How Data Is Used

Data accessed from QuickBooks Online is used exclusively for the following internal purposes:

  • Automating the creation of journal entries, invoices, and reconciliation records
  • Syncing transaction data from internal operational management systems
  • Generating internal financial summaries and reports

4. Data Storage & Retention

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online may be temporarily stored in internal databases for processing and reconciliation purposes. This data is retained only as long as necessary for accounting operations and is not used for marketing, analytics, or any unrelated purpose.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose any data obtained through the QuickBooks Online API to any third party. Data is used exclusively within our internal systems for the purposes described above.

6. Security

The Application uses OAuth 2.0 to authenticate with QuickBooks Online. Access tokens are stored securely and are never exposed publicly. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

7. Not a Public Application

This Application is strictly for internal use by CodeHobo. It is not available to consumers or the general public, does not accept user registrations, and collects no personal data from website visitors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
